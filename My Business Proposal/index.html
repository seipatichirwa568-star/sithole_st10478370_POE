Welcome to Isipho Donations

Isipho Donations is a South African non-profit organisation dedicated to improving the lives of disadvantaged individuals and families.

We work with communities, volunteers, schools, churches, businesses, and donors to provide support to people in need.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve lives by connecting donors with communities in need through transparent, reliable, and compassionate charitable services.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create stronger communities where everyone has access to basic necessities and opportunities for a better life.
