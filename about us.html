Organisation Overview

Isipho Donations is a South African non-profit organisation focused on helping disadvantaged individuals and families.

Our organisation works with volunteers, schools, churches, businesses, and community members to identify areas where assistance is needed.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve the lives of people in need by providing essential resources and connecting communities with generous donors.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create communities where people support one another and everyone has access to basic necessities.

Our Values

  • Compassion
  • Honesty
  • Transparency
  • Community
  • Reliability
