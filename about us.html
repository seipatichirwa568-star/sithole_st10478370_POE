WHO WE ARE

We believe in the power of showing up.

Isipho Donations is a non-profit organisation concept created for a web development project. Its purpose is to show how community donations can support people and families facing difficulties.

Our approach centres on individuals, schools, churches, local organisations and volunteers working together so that useful items reach those who need them.

This website demonstrates how a community-focused organisation could communicate its services and encourage people to get involved.

01 · OUR MISSION

Share practical support.

To connect caring donors with communities through food, clothing and learning resources, with compassion and transparency.

02 · OUR VISION

Stronger together.

A community where neighbours feel supported and opportunities are created through small, consistent acts of care.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

What items could be donated?

Food parcels, clean clothing and school stationery are examples. Use the enquiry form to ask about current needs and arrangements.

Can students volunteer?

The project includes a volunteer enquiry feature. Availability and any real-world volunteering arrangements would need confirmation from an operating organisation.

Is this an operating charity?

This is a student portfolio website concept. Contact and programme information must be verified before presenting it as a live charity service.
